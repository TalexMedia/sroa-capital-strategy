How SROA Capital could use it

One renter an episode, drawn from your properties across 31 states, tells their own story about the move that brought them in. The store manager who already knows the renter is the guide. Your renters have written more than 22,000 five-star reviews, and the series turns those reviews into stories people can watch.

Why long-form episodic wins over time
One-off videos are not appointment content. A returning series is what people come back for, and every move-in at your properties is a new episode waiting.
Trust is built in small moments repeated over time, and each episode is another opportunity to build trust.
More than 22,000 five-star reviews tell people your renters are happy. A renter telling their own story is what earns the next one's trust.

How SROA Capital could use it

One unit size an episode gets one honest question, answered by filling the unit for real. One episode shows what actually fits in a ten-by-ten. Another shows what a climate-controlled unit does to a box of photographs over a summer, and a third shows how far a five-by-five gets a student. The manager who rents these units every day runs the test, and every renter wants the answer before they book.

Why a short run of real tests works
Entertainment is value given first. Nobody passes around a unit-size chart, but people watch someone actually fill a ten-by-ten and find out.
Answer the question they already have. Every renter is trying to work out what fits before they book, and a test answers it better than a paragraph can.
The world resets every episode. There is another unit size, another season and another question, so the series never runs out of tests to run.

The Challenge
In 2020, Elizabeth Seton Children's launched a capital campaign to fund a $156 million young adult care facility for patients aging out of their pediatric program. They needed video content that would inspire donors, communicate urgency, and sustain momentum across a multi-year campaign.
The Approach
Talex Media produced multiple pieces of informational, inspirational, and commercial fundraising content across the campaign lifecycle. Each piece was designed to give donors a genuine connection to the mission and the people it serves, not just a request for money.
The Result
In August 2025, Elizabeth Seton Children's broke ground on their new 96-bed young adult center in White Plains, NY, the first standalone facility of its kind in the United States, providing care for young adults aged 18 to 35 with complex medical needs. The facility is expected to open in January 2028.

What happens after the call?
If there's a fit, we send a formal proposal tailored to your team, your community, and the goals you described. If not, you leave with a clearer picture of your content opportunity and I wish you well.
How is your approach different from a typical video agency?
We start with the community, not the camera. Before any production decision, we map who you're speaking to, what they need from you, and what shift we're trying to produce in them. Format and craft follow from there.
Do you only work with certain industries?
At Talex, we don’t limit our focus to one vertical or industry, because industry-specific thinking leads to uniform outputs and our goal is to help our clients stand out from their competition.

Ultimately, a cross-disciplinary perspective forces innovation and creates more original ideas. Because we are not tied to the conventions of one industry, we can do a better job of helping you create content authentic to your brand and valuable to your audience. So you stand out and remain top of mind for your customers.

Perfect, that's actually how we prefer to work. Talex Media isn't a marketing agency. We're a video production company in the documentary and broadcast tradition. A strong marketing team brings audience clarity, channel strategy, and distribution.
